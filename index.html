OpenClaw Personal Assistant

This is a private, single-user assistant run by Hunter House. It connects to the owner's own Google account so the assistant can read and send email and read and update calendar events on the owner's behalf.

The application is not offered to the public. No one other than the owner can sign in to it, and it is not a product or service for others to use.

What it does

  • Reads, searches, and sends Gmail messages in the owner's mailbox.
  • Reads, creates, and updates events in the owner's Google Calendar.
